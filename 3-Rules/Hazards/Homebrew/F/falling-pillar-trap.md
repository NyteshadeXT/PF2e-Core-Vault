```pf2e-stats
# Falling Pillar Trap
## Hazard 11

---

==Uncommon== ==Magical== ==Trap==

**Stealth** DC 30 (expert) to notice the faintly glowing runes worked into the pillar’s base and ceiling socket.

**Description** A massive stone pillar is held aloft by a magical restraint. The ward recognizes a creator-chosen identifying mark—such as an Ebon Triad sigil—allowing its bearers to pass safely.

**Disable** Thievery DC 30 (expert) to disrupt the release mechanism, or Arcana DC 30 (expert) to counteract the trap (counteract rank 6, counteract DC 30).

**AC** 31; **Hardness** 20; **HP** 80 (BT 40)  
**Immunities** object immunities

---

**Crushing Collapse** `pf2:r`  
**Trigger** A creature not bearing the trap’s identifying ward moves within 5 feet of the pillar.  
**Effect** The pillar crashes down. Each creature within 5 feet of it must attempt a DC 30 Reflex save, taking `dice: 10d10` bludgeoning damage with a basic Reflex save. A creature that critically fails is also knocked prone beneath the fallen pillar.

**Reset** Manual. The pillar must be raised and its magical restraint restored, requiring 1 hour of work and a successful DC 30 Crafting or Arcana check.

*Source: Homebrew*
```
