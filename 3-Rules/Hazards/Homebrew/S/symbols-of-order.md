```pf2e-stats
# Symbols of Order
## Hazard 12

---

==aura== ==emotion== ==magical== ==mental== ==order==

**Description** While the doors remain closed, the symbols project a 20-foot aura. A **chaos-attuned** creature that enters the aura or starts its turn there must attempt a Will save against the door’s DC.

### **Order Glyph** `pf2:r` 
**Trigger** A creature comes within 20 feet of the symbol or reads it.
**Effect** The symbol erupts with overwhelming dread. All creatures in a 20-foot emanation must attempt a DC 32 Will save.
- **Critical Success** The creature is unaffected and temporarily immune for 24 hours.   
- **Success** The creature is [[clumsy|clumsy 2]] while it remains within 20 feet of the doors and can see them.    
- **Failure** The creature can’t voluntarily move closer to the doors for 1 minute. It can attempt a new Will save at the end of each of its turns; on a success, the effect ends.  
- **Critical Failure** As failure, and the creature is [[clumsy|clumsy 2]] while it can see the closed doors.
  
The effect ends immediately if the doors open, the symbols are destroyed, or the creature is no longer chaos-attuned.
```
